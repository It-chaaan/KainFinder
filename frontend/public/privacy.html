Crave It is a food discovery prototype for Metro Manila. This notice describes the current browser-based search flow.

Location and search

Your browser asks for location only when you choose “Use my location.” You can deny that request and choose a city center or tap the map instead. The selected center and search text stay in the current page session; this prototype does not save them to a Crave It account or database.

Loading the map connects your browser to Google. When you press Search, your query and selected center are sent directly from your browser to Google Maps Platform for Places search. Crave It shows the returned place names, addresses, coordinates, pins, and approximate straight-line distances in the current page. It does not cache or store Google place content.

Google services and Directions

Google may process information from your browser under the Google Privacy Policy. Selecting Directions opens Google Maps in a new tab and sends the destination place ID and coordinates to Google. Google Maps then handles your route request; Crave It does not calculate or store a route.
