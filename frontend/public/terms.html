Crave It is a prototype that suggests food places around a point you choose in Metro Manila. A chosen city is a search center, not a strict city filter. A dish search suggests places; it does not confirm that a dish is available on a menu.

Place information

Place details come from Google Maps Platform and can change. Distances shown in Crave It are straight-line estimates, not walking or driving distance or travel time. Check the place and its current details before visiting.

Google Maps

Map and Places features use Google services and are subject to the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service and Google Terms of Service. Directions opens Google Maps in a new tab; Google provides any route, not Crave It. Google's Privacy Policy describes its data handling.
